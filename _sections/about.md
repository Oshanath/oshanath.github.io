# About

I'm Oshanath, a software engineer drawn to the space between
**well-built software** and **interesting research**. I work on API management
at WSO2 and explore secure computing, databases, and hardware acceleration.

I like understanding how things work, from the abstractions we write software
with to the hardware underneath. The most interesting problems, to me, are the
ones that connect those layers.

My background is in **Computer Science & Engineering at the University of
Moratuwa**. These days, I'm especially interested in homomorphic encryption
and making computation over encrypted data more practical.
