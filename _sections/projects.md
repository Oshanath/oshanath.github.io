# Projects

### [Number Theoretic Transform in hardware](https://github.com/Oshanath/hardware-number-theoretic-transform)
*Verilog · NTT · Hardware design*
An NTT, inverse NTT, and polynomial multiplier in Verilog. Three architectures
explore the trade-off between parallelism, latency, and hardware area:
sequential, combinational, and a mix of both.

### [STM32 peripheral drivers](https://github.com/Oshanath/STM32F1xxDrivers)
*C · STM32 · Embedded*
A collection of drivers for the STM32F1 family of microcontrollers, exploring
embedded software closer to the hardware.
